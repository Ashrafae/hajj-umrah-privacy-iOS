Privacy Policy — Hajj & Umrah Guide (iOS)

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Hajj & Umrah Guide (“we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information on iOS. The app is a free educational companion for pilgrims. It works without user accounts and is suitable for families.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 No Account Required

All main features are available without registration.

2.2 Voluntary Feedback Only

If you contact us, we may receive:

  • Your email address (to reply)
  • The content of your message
  • Date/time of submission

2.3 Automatically Collected Information (Non-Personal)

  • Device & OS: Basic model and iOS version for compatibility.
  • Anonymous analytics: Aggregate usage metrics to improve performance and features.
  • Crash reports: Technical diagnostics to find and fix bugs.

2.4 No Sensitive or Personal Data

  • No precise location (no GPS collection)
  • No camera/photos access
  • No contacts/files access
  • No social media integrations
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA) and no tracking

3. How We Use Information

Privacy by design: The app functions without personal data.

3.1 Feedback

  • Respond to your questions and support requests
  • Improve features and content
  • Fix reported issues

3.2 Anonymous Analytics

  • Monitor stability and performance
  • Understand which features help most
  • Guide roadmap and quality improvements

3.3 We Don’t

  • Run ads or ad trackers
  • Build behavioral profiles
  • Sell or share personal data

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information.

4.1 Limited Disclosures

  • Compliance with law or valid legal requests
  • Technical troubleshooting (e.g., anonymized crash logs)
  • Protecting security and preventing abuse

4.2 No Ads

No advertising partners and no ad tracking.

5. Data Security

5.1 Feedback Data

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Secure storage with limited access
  • Retention: feedback is deleted after 2 years unless needed for ongoing support

5.2 General Measures

  • No user accounts (reduced risk)
  • Minimal data collection
  • Regular maintenance and security updates
  • Anonymous analytics that cannot identify you

6. Your Privacy Rights

If you sent feedback, you can request access, correction, or deletion by emailing hajjumrahguide4you@gmail.com.

7. Children’s Privacy & Family-Friendly Content

  • Educational content vetted for suitability
  • No inappropriate content
  • No ads or ad tracking

8. iOS-Specific Notes

  • No Tracking: We do not track across apps/websites and do not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission.
  • No IDFA: We do not use the Apple advertising identifier.
  • Microphone/Audio: The app may access the audio session to handle playback interruptions. We do not record or store audio.
  • Background Audio: Enabled solely for continuous playback of approved content.
  • Diagnostics: Apple and TestFlight may provide anonymized crash/usage reports to the developer to improve stability.
  • Push Notifications: Not used.
  • Health, Location, Contacts, Photos: Not used.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Material changes will be highlighted in the app or on this page. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

Questions? Contact us at hajjumrahguide4you@gmail.com.
